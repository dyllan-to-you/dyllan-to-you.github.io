What is Solarpunk?

I like to explain Solarpunk by first talking about Cyberpunk
My understanding of Cyberpunk is that in the 70s and 80s SciFi authors were looking at technology and looking at society, and trying to project forward what they thought society would look like in 40-50 years. 

Unfortunately, Manifestation is Real. By defining a cyberpunk future, they brought it into Reality. We are now living in that cyberpunk dystopia those SciFi writers were trying to warn us about.
